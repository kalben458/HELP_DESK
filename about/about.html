About Us

Limay Senior High School (LSHS)
Limay, Bataan, Philippines
The LSHS Help Desk is a support platform created to assist students, teachers, and staff in reporting school-related concerns. Our goal is to provide a safe and accessible space where members of the school community can voice their concerns and complaints.
We believe that every concern matters. Whether it is about facilities. The Help Desk ensures that reports are properly received and addressed.
Our Mission:
To improve the school environment by listening, responding, and taking action on reported concerns in a fair and timely manner.
You can report:
  • School facility problems
  • Plumbing Concerns
  • Carpentry Issues
  • Electrical Concerns
All reports are handled responsibly and confidentially to ensure the safety and comfort of everyone involved.
